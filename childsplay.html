CHILD'S PLAY

Eight films. One very bad doll.

Child's Play (1988)

The one where Chucky is born

A serial killer transfers his soul into a Good Guy doll. The doll, Chucky, finds his way to young Andy Barclay and the bodies start piling up. Tom Holland directs; Brad Dourif voices Chucky.

Child's Play 2 (1990)

The one in the toy factory

Chucky is rebuilt and hunts Andy again, this time at a foster home. Ends in a massive doll-factory showdown.

Child's Play 3 (1991)

The one at military school

Teenage Andy at a military academy; Chucky mails himself there. The weakest of the original trilogy but sets up the later meta turn.

Bride of Chucky (1998)

The one with Tiffany

Chucky's ex-girlfriend Tiffany is resurrected into a doll; the two go on a road-trip killing spree. Shifts the series toward dark comedy and self-awareness.

Seed of Chucky (2004)

The one with Glen/Glenda

Chucky and Tiffany's child, Glen (or Glenda), enters the picture. The most camp entry, leaning fully into comedy.

Curse of Chucky (2013)

The one that goes back to horror

A direct-to-video return to straight horror: Chucky mailed to a grieving family. Re-centers the franchise on scares over jokes.

Cult of Chucky (2017)

The one with multiple Chuckys

Chucky learns to split his soul into multiple dolls. Nica Pierce returns; the ending sets up the TV series.

Child's Play (2019)

The one that's a reboot

A tech-age remake: a Buddi doll's safety protocols are disabled. Chucky is an AI gone rogue rather than a possessed toy. Separate continuity from the original series.
