FRIDAY THE 13TH

Twelve films. One mask. A lot of bad decisions at Camp Crystal Lake.

Friday the 13th (1980)

The one where the killer isn't Jason

The original. Camp Crystal Lake reopens, and a killer picks off the counselors one by one. The murderer is Pamela Voorhees, avenging her drowned son — Jason himself only appears in the final jump scare.

Friday the 13th Part 2 (1981)

The one with the bag over his head

Jason's first outing as the killer. He wears a burlap sack with one eyehole, not yet the hockey mask. Introduces Ginny, the counselor who fights back using psychology.

Friday the 13th Part III (1982)

The one where he gets the mask

Shot in 3D. Jason takes the iconic hockey mask off a victim and it becomes his signature. First film where Jason is the lone antagonist throughout.

Friday the 13th: The Final Chapter (1984)

The one where Tommy kills him

Jason is finally killed — by young Tommy Jarvis, who shaves his head to look like young Jason and hacks him down. The "final" chapter that wasn't.

Friday the 13th: A New Beginning (1985)

The one with the fake Jason

Jason is dead, so the killings are done by an impostor: Roy Burns, a paramedic who snaps after his son is murdered. The twist is the whole point.

Friday the 13th Part VI: Jason Lives (1986)

The one where he's resurrected

Tommy Jarvis accidentally brings Jason back to life with a lightning strike. Jason becomes an undead, near-invincible zombie for the rest of the series. More self-aware, almost comedic tone.

Friday the 13th Part VII: The New Blood (1988)

The one with the telekinetic girl

A teenage girl with Carrie-style telekinetic powers accidentally frees Jason from his watery grave. She's the one who can finally match him physically.

Friday the 13th Part VIII: Jason Takes Manhattan (1989)

The one on the boat (and briefly in Manhattan)

Jason hitches a ride on a high school graduation cruise to New York. Despite the title, most of it takes place on the ship; the Manhattan scenes are brief.

Jason Goes to Hell: The Final Friday (1993)

The one with the body-hopping worm

Jason is blown apart by the FBI, then his spirit possesses people via a parasitic worm passed mouth-to-mouth. Ends with Freddy Krueger's glove dragging the mask down.

Jason X (2001)

The one in space

Jason is cryogenically frozen and thawed in the 25th century aboard a spaceship. Gets upgraded into "Uber Jason" with a metal exoskeleton. Campy sci-fi.

Freddy vs. Jason (2003)

The one with Freddy Krueger

The crossover. Freddy resurrects Jason to spread fear in Springwood, then the two slashers turn on each other. The dream vs. the unstoppable.

Friday the 13th (2009)

The one that's a reboot

A Platinum Dunes remake that compresses the first four films into one: Mrs. Voorhees' revenge, Jason with the bag, then the mask. Faster, meaner Jason.
