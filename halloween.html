HALLOWEEN

Thirteen films. One Shape. One very long Halloween night.

Halloween (1978)

The one that started it all

John Carpenter's original. Michael Myers escapes the asylum and stalks babysitter Laurie Strode on Halloween night in Haddonfield. The Shape is born.

Halloween II (1981)

The one in the hospital

Picks up the same night. Michael follows Laurie to the hospital; the big twist reveals they're siblings. Laurie's last appearance for 17 years.

Halloween III: Season of the Witch (1982)

The one without Michael Myers

A standalone story: a toymaker's Halloween masks kill children via a Stonehenge-powered broadcast. No Michael, no Laurie. Divisive then, cult favorite now.

Halloween 4: The Return of Michael Myers (1988)

The one with Jamie Lloyd

Michael escapes again and hunts his niece Jamie. Ends with Jamie stabbing her foster mother — implying the evil has passed to her.

Halloween 5: The Revenge of Michael Myers (1989)

The one with the psychic link

Jamie can sense Michael's thoughts and location. Introduces the mysterious Man in Black, who breaks Michael out at the end.

Halloween: The Curse of Michael Myers (1995)

The one with the cult of Thorn

Explains Michael's immortality via an ancient druid curse. Tommy Jarvis (yes, same name) returns as an adult. Messy production, multiple cuts.

Halloween H20: 20 Years Later (1998)

The one where Laurie comes back

Laurie Strode, now a headmistress, faces Michael again. She decapitates him at the end — seemingly for good.

Halloween: Resurrection (2002)

The one with the reality TV show

Michael's death is retconned; he kills Laurie in the opening. The rest is a webcast exploring his childhood home. Busta Rhymes fights Michael.

Halloween (2007)

The one that's a remake

Rob Zombie's reimagining. Expands Michael's childhood and backstory; more brutal and grounded than Carpenter's original.

Halloween II (2009)

The one with the visions

Zombie's sequel. Laurie has visions of her mother; Michael is haunted by his own. Ends with Laurie apparently taking on the Shape's role.

Halloween (2018)

The one that ignores everything after 1978

Direct sequel to the original only — no sibling twist. Laurie, now a survivalist grandma, traps Michael in her burning basement.

Halloween Kills (2021)

The one where the town fights back

Michael escapes the fire and cuts through Haddonfield. The townsfolk form a mob — which doesn't go well for them. "Evil dies tonight."

Halloween Ends (2022)

The one with Corey Cunningham

Four years later. A bullied teen is corrupted by Michael's evil. The final showdown between Laurie and Michael closes the trilogy.
