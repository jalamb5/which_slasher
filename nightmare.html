A NIGHTMARE ON ELM STREET

Nine films. One dream demon. Don't fall asleep.

A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984)

The one that introduced Freddy

Wes Craven's original. Freddy Krueger, a burned child killer, murders teens in their dreams. "One, two, Freddy's coming for you…"

A Nightmare on Elm Street 2: Freddy's Revenge (1985)

The one where Freddy possesses a teen

Freddy tries to escape the dream world by possessing Jesse Walsh. Notable for its homoerotic subtext, which has made it a cult classic.

A Nightmare on Elm Street 3: Dream Warriors (1987)

The one where the kids fight back

Teens in a psych ward learn to use their dream powers against Freddy. Nancy returns. "Welcome to prime time, bitch!"

A Nightmare on Elm Street 4: The Dream Master (1988)

The one with Alice

Alice gains the dream powers of her friends as Freddy kills them, eventually becoming the Dream Master and trapping him in a mirror.

A Nightmare on Elm Street 5: The Dream Child (1989)

The one via the unborn baby

Freddy uses Alice's unborn child's dreams to reach new victims. Darker, more gothic tone; Freddy's origin is expanded.

Freddy's Dead: The Final Nightmare (1991)

The one in 3D

Freddy's last stand, with a daughter he never knew about. Shot partly in 3D; features cameos from Johnny Depp and others.

Wes Craven's New Nightmare (1994)

The one that's meta

Freddy escapes into the real world and haunts the actress who played Nancy. A precursor to Scream's self-aware horror.

Freddy vs. Jason (2003)

The one with Jason Voorhees

Freddy resurrects Jason to spread fear in Springwood, then the two slashers turn on each other. The crossover.

A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010)

The one that's a remake

Platinum Dunes reboot with Jackie Earle Haley as Freddy. Darker backstory; Freddy is more sinister, less quippy.
