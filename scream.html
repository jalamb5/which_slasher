SCREAM

Seven films. Do you like scary movies?

Scream (1996)

The one that changed everything

Wes Craven's meta-slasher: a killer in a Ghostface mask stalks teens in Woodsboro who know the rules of horror movies. Reinvented the genre.

Scream 2 (1997)

The one at college

Sidney Prescott at university; a copycat killer strikes. Sequel rules apply — bigger, bloodier, with a media-circus subplot.

Scream 3 (2000)

The one in Hollywood

The killer targets the cast of "Stab 3" on a movie set. Sidney confronts her past; the trilogy's secrets come out.

Scream 4 (2011)

The one with the new generation

Sidney returns to Woodsboro for a book tour; a new Ghostface targets a new group of teens. Remake-era commentary.

Scream (2022)

The one that's a requel

A new killer in Woodsboro draws the old guard back. "Requel" rules: legacy characters plus new blood, with a meta take on fandom.

Scream VI (2023)

The one in New York

The survivors relocate to New York City; Ghostface follows. The first film without Sidney Prescott as lead.

Scream 7 (2026)

The one that brings Sidney back

Sidney Prescott returns to face a new Ghostface. Kevin Williamson directs.
